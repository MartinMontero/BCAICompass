# Proposed ten-organization pilot

These are review candidates, not approved imports or a claim that either project has never researched them. Source URLs are in `pilot-review-candidates.csv`.

1. UBC Data Science Institute
2. TrustML
3. Human-AI Interaction @ UBC (HAI)
4. Trustworthy Artificial Intelligence (TAI) Lab
5. iViz Lab
6. Metacreation Lab for Creative AI
7. BCNET
8. New Ventures BC
9. AI@UVic
10. Kootenay Association for Science & Technology (KAST)

Martin and BC + AI would first agree the ten candidates; swaps are welcome. Each project would review its own record and publication decisions, resolve aliases and parent/lab/site distinctions, and check current primary sources before accepting additions or corrections.

Record one identity disposition per candidate: same entity, distinct entity, lab/parent, facility/operator, unresolved, or excluded with a reason. Keep entity kind, operating status, geography, inclusion rationale and claim evidence distinct. Suggested routes or shared cities must not become partnership edges.

Success means ten documented dispositions, current evidence for accepted facts, preserved attribution, no duplicate parent/lab/site records, and a traceable source release for each accepted import. An unresolved or excluded outcome is useful. If both projects accept an addition or correction, exercise one end-to-end exchange in each direction before expanding the process. No increase in record count or reciprocal commitment is assumed.
